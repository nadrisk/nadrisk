## Preparation

The Freesound high-quality previews were converted to 44.1 kHz, 16-bit WAV, DC-centred, peak-normalized with headroom, trimmed, and given short edge fades. `rave-amen.wav` remains exactly 13.333 seconds long at 180 BPM so BRK can tempo-match its full 40-beat performance.

No audio from the original Winstons recording, T99, Second Phase, Kevin Saunderson, James Brown, Lyn Collins, or other commercial masters is bundled. Those famous sounds informed the categories only.
